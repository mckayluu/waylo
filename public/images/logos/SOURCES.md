# Platform marks

These are monochrome SVGs from Simple Icons v16.37.0 (https://simpleicons.org, icon data CC0 1.0), fetched from https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@16.37.0/icons/<slug>.svg on Nov 12, 2026.

The trademarks belong to their owners. We use them unaltered and in a single color, with clear space around each one. They appear only to show platforms where the Waylo script tag can be added. None of these companies partners with Waylo or endorses it, and Waylo has no native app for any of them.

Slack isn't in Simple Icons, because it was removed at the brand owner's request. Slack's guidelines say not to recolor its logo. Until we use an official asset from Slack's media kit, the homepage shows a "Slack" text tile instead. CRM is the text tile "Any CRM via webhook". Waylo has no native HubSpot or Salesforce app, only a JSON webhook.
